Program name

Checkpoint, operated by Secret Soup Company.

Program description

Checkpoint is an interactive ambient art project. Participants opt in by physically tapping an NFC sticker on a real-world object after receiving an in-person explanation of the project. Tapping the sticker composes a pre-written SMS that the participant chooses to send to our number; sending that text constitutes consent to receive replies. Once opted in, you may receive conversational text replies and occasional check-in messages from the project.

Message frequency

Frequency varies. Messages are sent conversationally in response to your texts, plus occasional project-wide check-ins. Typical participants receive a small number of messages per month.
