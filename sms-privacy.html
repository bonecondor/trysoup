SMS Privacy Policy

This privacy policy covers the SMS messaging program operated by Secret Soup Company in connection with the Checkpoint art project. It applies only to text messages exchanged with our published phone number.

What we collect

When you text our number we collect: your phone number, the content of the messages you send and receive, the date and time of each message, and which of our physical NFC stickers initiated the conversation (when known).

How we use it

We use this information solely to participate in the Checkpoint project with you: to send relevant replies, to remember our prior conversation so future replies make sense, and to keep an internal log of project activity.

What we don't do

We do not share your phone number or message content with third parties for their own purposes. We do not sell this data. We do not use this data for marketing of any kind. We do not use it for advertising profiling.

Service providers

We use Twilio as the underlying SMS carrier. Twilio processes message content as required to deliver SMS to and from your phone, under Twilio's own privacy practices.

Opting out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to the number. Your phone number will be retained only as a record that you opted out, so we do not contact you again.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, write to t@trysoup.xyz.
