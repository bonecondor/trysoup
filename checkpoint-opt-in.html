Checkpoint Opt-In Evidence

This page documents the public opt-in flow for Checkpoint, an interactive SMS art project by Secret Soup Company.

Overview

Participants opt in only in person through physical NFC-tagged objects or stickers distributed at real-world activations, installations, and events. The participant must manually send the first text message from the participant's own device. No one is enrolled automatically.

Participants encounter a physical Checkpoint object or sticker in person.

Step 3: The participant taps the sticker

Tapping the NFC sticker opens the participant's native SMS app with a pre-addressed, prefilled message to the Checkpoint phone number.

Step 4: The participant manually presses Send

The participant chooses whether to send the first text. The participant is not opted in unless the participant manually presses Send.

After opt-in

After the first text is sent, participants may receive conversational replies and occasional continuation or check-in messages related to the project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Participants can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
